# Cost Centers and Profit Centers

> SAMPLE / PLACEHOLDER POLICY — replace with the approved Controlling (CO) policy before pilot.

## Cost center vs profit center

A cost center (CO-CCA) collects costs for an organizational unit that does not
directly earn revenue — for example a team or a department. A profit center
(CO-PCA) measures profitability for a part of the business that has both revenue
and cost. Every cost center is assigned to exactly one profit center.

## Requesting a new cost center

Submit a Cost Center Request to the Controlling team with the responsible
manager, the hierarchy node it belongs under, the valid-from date, and the
profit center it rolls up to. Requests are processed weekly. Cost centers are
not deleted mid-year; they are set to blocked and end-dated at year end.

## Requesting a new profit center

Submit a Profit Center Request with the responsible manager, the segment it
belongs to, and the valid-from date. Profit centers are created in the current
controlling area and standard hierarchy.

## Allocations, assessments and distributions

Shared costs (for example facilities or IT) are allocated from the sending cost
center to receiving cost centers each month during close, using assessment
cycles on approved allocation bases (headcount, floor area, or usage).
Distributions keep the original cost element; assessments post to a secondary
assessment cost element. Allocation cycles are changed only at period start.

## Postings to a blocked or wrong cost center

A posting to a blocked cost center is rejected. A cost booked to the wrong cost
center is corrected with a reposting (transaction-based or via a CO document),
not a new AP or GL entry, and must stay within the same period where possible.

## Checking cost center actuals

Live cost center actual / plan lookups are not connected to this assistant yet.
Use the cost center report in S/4HANA or ask Controlling. This assistant can
explain CO master data and allocation policy now.
