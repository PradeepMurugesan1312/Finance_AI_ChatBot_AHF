# Purchase Order Approval Thresholds

> SAMPLE / PLACEHOLDER POLICY — replace with the approved procurement policy before pilot.

## Single purchase order approval limits

| Total PO value (USD)        | Required approver                          |
|-----------------------------|--------------------------------------------|
| Up to 2,500                 | Requester's line manager                   |
| 2,501 – 25,000              | Department head                            |
| 25,001 – 100,000            | Divisional VP                              |
| 100,001 – 500,000           | CFO                                        |
| Over 500,000                | CFO and CEO, plus board notification       |

Approvals are sequential: each tier also requires all lower-tier approvals.

## Purchase requisition vs purchase order

A purchase requisition (PR) is the internal request to buy. It must be approved
before Procurement converts it into a purchase order (PO) sent to the supplier.
PR approval follows the same value tiers as above.

## Splitting is prohibited

Breaking one purchase into multiple smaller POs or PRs to stay under an approval
tier is a policy violation. Recurring spend with the same supplier is assessed
on the annual total, not the individual PO.

## Emergency purchases

For genuine business emergencies outside business hours, a Divisional VP may
verbally approve up to 50,000. The verbal approval must be recorded in the
system within two business days with a written justification.

## Three-way match

Invoices are paid only when the PO, the goods/service receipt, and the supplier
invoice match on quantity and price within tolerance (see the invoice payment
terms policy for tolerance values). A failed three-way match blocks the invoice
for payment until Procurement or the requester resolves it.
