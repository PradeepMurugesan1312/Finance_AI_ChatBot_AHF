# Bank and Cash Management (TR-CM)

> SAMPLE / PLACEHOLDER POLICY — replace with the approved Treasury / Bank Accounting policy before pilot.

## Bank statement import and reconciliation

Electronic bank statements are imported every morning for the prior day. The
system auto-matches statement lines to open items and payment/clearing
documents. Unmatched lines are posted to a bank clearing (bank sub-account) and
cleared manually by Treasury within one business day. A bank sub-account with
items older than five business days is escalated.

## House banks and bank accounts

A house bank represents a bank the company holds accounts with; each account is
a bank account in Bank Account Management. Opening or closing a bank account is
a Treasury request that also needs CFO sign-off and updates to the payment
program configuration and signatory list.

## Cash position and liquidity

The daily cash position is built from cleared bank balances plus known
same-day flows. The liquidity forecast extends it using AP due dates, AR
expected receipts, and planned treasury items. Both are Treasury reports; the
forecast is not a commitment.

## Payment runs

The automatic payment program runs Tuesday and Friday. It selects due, approved,
unblocked open items, groups them by payee and payment method, and creates the
payment and clearing documents plus the bank file. A payment proposal is
reviewed and released by Treasury before the run posts.

## Petty cash

Petty cash floats are capped at 500 USD per location, reconciled monthly, and
replenished against itemized receipts. Cash advances to employees go through
payroll or T&E, not petty cash.

## Checking a bank balance or statement

Live bank-balance and statement lookups are not connected to this assistant
yet. Use the bank reconciliation or cash management report in S/4HANA, or ask
Treasury. This assistant can explain bank and cash process now.
