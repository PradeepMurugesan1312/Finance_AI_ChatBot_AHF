# Travel and Expense (T&E) Policy

> SAMPLE / PLACEHOLDER POLICY — replace with the approved T&E policy before pilot.

## Submission deadline

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the trip end date or the
purchase date. Reports submitted after 60 days require Department head approval
and may be treated as taxable income.

## Receipts

An itemized receipt is required for any single expense of 25 USD or more. Credit
card statements are not itemized receipts. Missing-receipt affidavits are
allowed twice per employee per fiscal year.

## Air travel

- Book through the corporate travel tool at least 14 days in advance where
  possible.
- Economy class for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for
  flights of 6 hours or more. Business class requires Divisional VP approval.
- Seat selection and one checked bag are reimbursable. Trip insurance and
  lounge access are not.

## Lodging

Reimbursed at actual reasonable cost. If the nightly rate exceeds the published
city cap by more than 20 percent, attach a justification. Room service and
in-room entertainment are not reimbursable.

## Meals

Per diem applies for trips of 24 hours or more; actual itemized cost otherwise,
capped at 75 USD per day domestic and 100 USD per day international. Alcohol is
not reimbursable.

## Mileage

Personal vehicle mileage is reimbursed at the current IRS standard rate.
Mileage and a rental car for the same leg of a trip cannot both be claimed.

## Non-reimbursable examples

Personal entertainment, traffic and parking fines, spouse or family travel,
personal grooming, loyalty program fees, and lost personal items.
