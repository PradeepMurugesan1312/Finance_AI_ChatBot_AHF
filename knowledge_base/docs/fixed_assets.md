# Fixed Assets (FI-AA)

> SAMPLE / PLACEHOLDER POLICY — replace with the approved Fixed Assets / capital policy before pilot.

## Capitalization threshold

An item is capitalized as a fixed asset when it costs 5,000 USD or more and has
a useful life over one year. Below the threshold it is expensed. Bulk purchases
of similar low-value items are assessed on the total, not the unit price, where
they form one functional unit.

## Asset classes and useful life

Standard useful lives: IT hardware 3 years, office furniture 7 years, leasehold
improvements over the lease term, buildings 30 years, plant and machinery 10
years. The asset class drives the depreciation account determination and
default life; overriding the default life needs Fixed Asset Accounting approval.

## Depreciation

Depreciation is straight-line, posted monthly by the depreciation run during
close. The depreciation start date is the first day of the month of
capitalization. A depreciation key change or unplanned depreciation needs
Controller approval.

## Assets under construction (AUC)

Capital project costs are collected on an AUC via an internal order or WBS
element. The AUC is not depreciated. On project completion it is settled to one
or more final assets, which then begin depreciating.

## Retirement and transfer

An asset retirement (scrapping or sale) needs the asset number, the retirement
date, and — for a sale — the proceeds and customer. An intra-company transfer
between cost centers or company codes is a Fixed Assets transaction, not a
manual journal entry. Physical disposal without a system retirement is a policy
violation.
