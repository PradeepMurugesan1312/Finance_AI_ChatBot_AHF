# General Ledger, Journal Entries and G/L Postings

> SAMPLE / PLACEHOLDER POLICY — replace with the approved GL / Record-to-Report policy before pilot.

## Requesting a new G/L account

Submit a G/L Account Request to the GL team with the account purpose, the
account group (balance sheet or P&L), the cost element type if it is a P&L
account, and the requesting cost center or profit center. New accounts are
created centrally in the chart of accounts and extended to the relevant company
codes. Standard turnaround is three business days.

## Parking vs posting a journal entry

A parked document is saved but has no accounting impact and can be edited or
deleted. A posted document updates balances and can only be corrected by a
reversal. Park an entry when it needs review or is missing information; post it
only when it is complete and approved.

## Manual journal entry approval

Manual journal entries above 10,000 USD require review by a second accountant
before posting. Entries above 100,000 USD also require Controller approval.
Recurring and accrual entries follow their own approved templates and do not
need per-run approval.

## Month-end close calendar

The accounting period closes on business day 4 (BD+4) of the following month.
Sub-ledgers (AP, AR, Assets) close on BD+2. After BD+4 the prior period is
locked; a post-close adjustment needs Controller approval and is booked to the
current open period unless a period reopen is explicitly granted.

## Document types and number ranges

Journal entries use document type SA. Vendor invoices use KR, customer invoices
DR, payments KZ / DZ, asset postings AA. Do not reuse a document type outside
its purpose — reporting and reconciliation rely on it.

## Intercompany postings

Intercompany journal entries must be booked with the intercompany trading
partner populated and mirrored in both company codes in the same period.
Unmatched intercompany balances are reconciled monthly before close.

Use the trial balance or the G/L line item report in S/4HANA, or ask the GL
