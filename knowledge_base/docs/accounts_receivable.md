# Accounts Receivable

> SAMPLE / PLACEHOLDER POLICY — replace with the approved AR / Order-to-Cash policy before pilot.

## Standard customer payment terms

Default customer terms are Net 30 from invoice date. Net 45 or Net 60 require
approval from the Credit Manager and must be recorded on the customer master.
New customers are invoiced on a prepayment or Net 15 basis until a credit
history is established.

## Credit limits

Every customer has a credit limit on the customer master. An order that would
push the customer over its limit is blocked and routed to the Credit Manager. A
limit increase needs a credit review (aging history, external credit score, and
account owner sign-off).

## Dunning and collections

Dunning runs weekly. The dunning levels are: level 1 at 15 days past due
(reminder), level 2 at 30 days (first formal notice), level 3 at 45 days
(final notice, delivery hold), level 4 at 60 days (referral to collections).
Disputed items are excluded from dunning until the dispute is resolved.

## Cash application

Incoming payments are applied against open invoices daily from the bank
statement. Unidentified receipts are posted to a clearing account and researched
within two business days. Short payments over tolerance (the lower of 2 percent
or 50 USD) are posted as a dispute, not written off.

## Bad debt write-off

A receivable is proposed for write-off after 120 days past due with collections
exhausted. Write-offs up to 5,000 USD are approved by the AR Manager; above that
by the Controller. Write-off does not stop collections activity.
